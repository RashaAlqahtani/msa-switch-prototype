Switching to Modern Standard Arabic

An in-car voice assistant drifts into Egyptian dialect. She asks for MSA by voice; it switches back, admits when it cannot keep to MSA, and remembers how she wants the switch acknowledged.

Implemented

  • Voice input (Web Speech API, keyword matching)
  • Speech output (Arabic and English voices)
  • Style change by voice, saved on the device
  • Memory of her choices across drives
  • Blind pairwise research comparison + JSON export

Simulated

  • Assistant replies are scripted
  • The dialect drift and the slip are scripted
  • "Next day" is simulated in the same session
  • Buttons simulate her speech when the mic cannot work (placeholder)

Planned

  • Replies generated by a language model in her chosen style
  • Automatic detection of dialect drift
  1. Optional: tick to skip voice and pauses.
  2. Press Start driving. The assistant drifts into Egyptian dialect.
  3. Ask for MSA: tap 🎤 and say "Please speak MSA" (or «تكلم بالفصحى»), or press Ask without the mic.
  4. After the apology, try changing the style: say "Don't apologise" (or press that small button).
  5. At the Decline turn, each of the three options leads to a different next day.
  6. Press Replay the drive: the assistant now uses the style you chose, without being told again.
  7. Optional: Research comparison (6 blind pairs, for the evaluation).
Grey notes inside the phone explain each step. Every Arabic line has an English translation (toggle with Hide English).
Hands-free: the phone sits on a dashboard mount, so she keeps her eyes on the road.
